# Topic 6.1: Decentralized Governance, veNEAR, and the House of Stake

The source of truth for lesson 6.1. The lesson script (`6.1.md`) and the code may only teach what this
file says. Facts carry a source tag; anything else is marked (unverified).

## In one sentence

Governance on NEAR combines validator staking delegation, the House of Stake (where locked NEAR becomes veNEAR voting power that grows the longer it stays locked) and on-chain decentralized autonomous organizations (AstroDAO / Sputnik DAO).

## The problem it solves

Pure "1-token-1-vote" governance models allow wealthy short-term speculators (or flash-loan attackers) to borrow tokens, pass proposals that benefit themselves at the expense of long-term network health, and immediately dump their tokens. Long-term stakeholders need governance mechanisms that reward sustained commitment and make a quick in-and-out impossible. [near-governance] [hos-docs]

## How it works on NEAR

**Validator Staking & Delegation.**
- NEAR secures its Proof-of-Stake consensus through active validator nodes. [near-staking]
- Any token holder can delegate NEAR to a validator staking pool without giving up custody of their funds. Delegators earn a share of the protocol's inflation rewards, a few percent a year (the rate moves with total stake; mainnet `max_inflation_rate` is 1/40 = 2.5% of supply a year). [near-staking] [mainnet-config]
- **Unstaking delay:** When unstaking, funds wait 4 epochs before they can be withdrawn. [near-staking] A mainnet epoch is 43,200 blocks (`epoch_length`), and blocks come about every 0.57 s (measured 13 Nov 2026 over 100,000 blocks), so an epoch is about 7 hours and 4 epochs a little over a day (about 28 hours). [mainnet-config]

**The House of Stake and veNEAR.** Checked against the contracts and the live mainnet contract, 13 Nov 2026.
- Users lock NEAR (or stNEAR / liNEAR) in a personal lockup contract. There is **no lock length to choose**: "a single flexible locking model that does not require users to commit to a fixed duration". [hos-docs]
- **Locked NEAR counts 1:1 at once.** Locking X NEAR gives X veNEAR straight away, and from then on the account "immediately start[s] to receive extra veNEAR for the new total locked NEAR amount". [hos-contracts]
- **Extra veNEAR grows at a fixed yearly rate, linearly** (a share of the locked NEAR per year, not compounding). The rate is set when the contract is deployed. On mainnet (`venear.dao`, `get_snapshot` → `venear_growth_config.FixedRate.annual_growth_rate_ns` = 15,850,000,000,000 / 10^30 per ns) it is **50% a year**: the production runbook says "50% per annum linear". [mainnet-venear] [hos-ops] (The repo's example deploy script uses 6% a year: that is not the live setting.) [hos-contracts]
- **Cap:** the House of Stake docs say veNEAR accrues "up to a maximum equivalent of 4 years of lock". [hos-docs] We could not find that cap in the contract's growth code (`common/src/venear.rs` is a plain rate × time × balance), so the lesson states it as "the docs say". At 50% a year, 4 years would make 100 locked NEAR worth 300 veNEAR.
- **Unlocking:** "When a user unlocks any amount of NEAR, the user forfeits all extra veNEAR amount accumulated during the lockup period." [hos-contracts] The NEAR being unlocked moves from the locked balance to a pending balance, so it stops counting too (`begin_unlock_near` in `lockup-contract/src/venear.rs`). [hos-contracts]
- **Unlock wait:** pending NEAR can be withdrawn only after `unlock_duration_ns`. On mainnet that is 3,888,000,000,000,000 ns = **45 days** (`venear.dao` `get_config`; the runbook says "45 days in ns"; the docs say "45 day cooldown"). [mainnet-venear] [hos-ops] [hos-docs]
- veNEAR is the voting power in the **House of Stake**, NEAR's token-holder governance (proposals on ecosystem funding and protocol direction). [near-governance] [hos-docs]
- **Contrast:** some other vote-escrow systems, e.g. Curve's, make you pick a lock length and scale power by it (up to 4 years for full power). House of Stake does not: power grows with time already spent locked, and leaving early costs the growth, not a broken promise. [curve-venear]

**On-Chain DAOs (AstroDAO & Sputnik).**
- Grassroots ecosystem initiatives, developer councils, and regional guilds organize as DAOs. [near-daos]
- Built on the Sputnik DAO smart contract framework (`AstroDAO`). [near-daos]
- Supports multi-signature voting, token-weighted voting, configurable quorum thresholds, and automated on-chain execution of approved payout proposals. [near-daos]

## Words to know

- **Delegation:** Assigning the voting and validation power of your tokens to a chosen validator node while retaining full asset ownership.
- **veNEAR (vote-escrow NEAR):** House of Stake voting power. 1 veNEAR for each locked NEAR, plus extra veNEAR that grows the longer the NEAR stays locked. Not a token you can trade.
- **House of Stake:** NEAR's token-holder governance, where people who lock NEAR vote with veNEAR.
- **AstroDAO / Sputnik DAO:** The modular smart contract standard for building on-chain DAOs on NEAR.
- **Unbonding period:** The wait after unstaking before funds can be withdrawn: 4 epochs, a little over a day on mainnet.
- **Unlock wait (House of Stake):** The wait after unlocking NEAR from veNEAR before it can be withdrawn: 45 days on mainnet.

## Common misunderstandings

- **"Delegating tokens transfers ownership to the validator."** No. Your tokens remain in the smart contract staking pool; the validator only receives validation weight. You can unstake at any time (subject to the unbonding period). [near-staking]
- **"All NEAR holders have equal voting power per token."** Liquid NEAR in a wallet has no House of Stake vote at all. 100 NEAR locked today counts 100 veNEAR; 100 NEAR that has stayed locked for 2 years counts 200 veNEAR at the mainnet rate. [hos-contracts] [mainnet-venear]
- **"You have to pick a lock length, like on Curve."** No. You lock with no end date and can start unlocking any time; the cost is losing the extra veNEAR and a 45-day wait. [hos-docs] [hos-contracts]
- **"A flash loan gets no voting power."** It does: borrowed NEAR, once locked, counts 1:1 at once. What stops the attack is that the NEAR cannot come back out within the loan: unlocking takes 45 days. [hos-contracts] [mainnet-venear]
- **"DAOs require manual off-chain payouts."** AstroDAO proposals execute directly on-chain upon reaching the required quorum. [near-daos]

## Compared with Ethereum & Cosmos

- **Ethereum Governance:** Off-chain "rough consensus" via core developer meetings and EIP discussions; token holders do not vote directly on protocol hard forks. DAO governance primarily runs on Snapshot (off-chain gasless signatures) with multi-sig execution (Safe). [eth-gov]
- **Cosmos:** Formal on-chain governance where staked ATOM directly votes on chain parameter updates and software upgrades with a 21-day unbonding period. [cosmos-gov]
- **NEAR:** Hybrid approach combining validator signaling, on-chain veNEAR stake governance, and modular AstroDAO organizations. [near-governance]

## Strengths and weaknesses (feeds the Honest take)

**Strengths:**
- Long-term alignment: voting power grows with time locked, and anyone who votes has NEAR that cannot leave for 45 days, so short-term borrowed tokens cannot vote and exit. [hos-contracts] [mainnet-venear]
- Flexible coordination: AstroDAO provides turnkey on-chain governance infrastructure with built-in multi-role permissions. [near-daos]
- Non-custodial staking: Delegated tokens never leave protocol custody. [near-staking]

**Weaknesses:**
- Illiquidity: locked NEAR needs 45 days to come out, so you cannot react quickly to a market crash or emergency, and unlocking any amount wipes all the extra veNEAR you built up. [hos-contracts] [mainnet-venear]
- Early lockers count more: at 50% a year, someone locked for 2 years has twice the power per NEAR of a newcomer. [mainnet-venear]
- Voter apathy: Like most decentralized governance systems, quorum can be difficult to reach without active foundation participation. [near-governance]

## What the lesson must show

1. View a live or recorded governance proposal (e.g. validator fee adjustment or community treasury grant).
2. The veNEAR calculator: lock 100 NEAR (100 veNEAR at once), then move a "time locked" slider from 0 to 4 years and watch the extra veNEAR grow at the mainnet rate (50% a year, up to the docs' 4-year cap: 300 veNEAR). Show that unlocking forfeits the extra and starts a 45-day wait.
3. Staking delegation overview: Inspect a list of validators, their commission rates, and uptime.
4. Cast a vote on the proposal (or simulate in demo mode).
5. Watch the vote tally update against the required quorum threshold.
