# Topic 6.1: Decentralized Governance, veNEAR, and the House of Stake

## In one sentence

Governance on NEAR combines validator staking delegation with the vote-escrow model (veNEAR in the House of Stake) and on-chain decentralized autonomous organizations (AstroDAO), aligning long-term token locking with decision-making power.

## The problem it solves

Pure "1-token-1-vote" governance models allow wealthy short-term speculators (or flash-loan attackers) to borrow tokens, pass proposals that benefit themselves at the expense of long-term network health, and immediately dump their tokens. Long-term stakeholders need governance mechanisms that reward sustained commitment. [near-governance] [curve-venear]

## How it works on NEAR

**Validator Staking & Delegation.**
- NEAR secures its Proof-of-Stake consensus through active validator nodes. [near-staking]
- Any token holder can delegate NEAR to a validator staking pool without giving up custody of their funds. Delegators earn protocol inflation rewards (~5% annually) while delegating their voting weight for protocol parameter updates. [near-staking]
- **Unstaking delay:** When unstaking, funds enter an unbonding period (approx. 4 epochs, or ~48 hours on mainnet) before becoming liquid. [near-staking]

**The Vote-Escrow Model (veNEAR & House of Stake).**
- Borrowed from Curve Finance's proven `ve` tokenomics architecture. [curve-venear]
- Users lock native NEAR tokens in escrow for a specified duration (ranging from a few months up to several years). [near-governance]
- In exchange, they receive **veNEAR** (voting-escrow NEAR). The longer the lock duration, the higher the voting power multiplier per locked token. [near-governance]
- veNEAR grants direct voting rights in the **House of Stake**, directing ecosystem grants, incentive emissions, and protocol parameters. [near-governance]

**On-Chain DAOs (AstroDAO & Sputnik).**
- Grassroots ecosystem initiatives, developer councils, and regional guilds organize as DAOs. [near-daos]
- Built on the Sputnik DAO smart contract framework (`AstroDAO`). [near-daos]
- Supports multi-signature voting, token-weighted voting, configurable quorum thresholds, and automated on-chain execution of approved payout proposals. [near-daos]

## Words to know

- **Delegation:** Assigning the voting and validation power of your tokens to a chosen validator node while retaining full asset ownership.
- **Vote-Escrow (veNEAR):** A mechanism where voting power is proportional to both the quantity of tokens locked and the duration of the lockup period.
- **House of Stake:** The governance body on NEAR representing locked token holders and validator interests.
- **AstroDAO / Sputnik DAO:** The modular smart contract standard for building on-chain DAOs on NEAR.
- **Unbonding Period:** The mandatory waiting time (~48 hours / 4 epochs) required after unstaking before funds can be transferred.

## Common misunderstandings

- **"Delegating tokens transfers ownership to the validator."** No. Your tokens remain in the smart contract staking pool; the validator only receives validation weight. You can unstake at any time (subject to the unbonding period). [near-staking]
- **"All NEAR holders have equal voting power per token."** In the House of Stake, someone locking 100 NEAR for 4 years has significantly more voting influence than someone holding 100 liquid NEAR in a wallet. [curve-venear]
- **"DAOs require manual off-chain payouts."** AstroDAO proposals execute directly on-chain upon reaching the required quorum. [near-daos]

## Compared with Ethereum & Cosmos

- **Ethereum Governance:** Off-chain "rough consensus" via core developer meetings and EIP discussions; token holders do not vote directly on protocol hard forks. DAO governance primarily runs on Snapshot (off-chain gasless signatures) with multi-sig execution (Safe). [eth-gov]
- **Cosmos:** Formal on-chain governance where staked ATOM directly votes on chain parameter updates and software upgrades with a 21-day unbonding period. [cosmos-gov]
- **NEAR:** Hybrid approach combining validator signaling, on-chain veNEAR stake governance, and modular AstroDAO organizations. [near-governance]

## Strengths and weaknesses (feeds the Honest take)

**Strengths:**
- Long-term alignment: veNEAR ensures decision-makers have a long-term economic stake in the protocol's success. [curve-venear]
- Flexible coordination: AstroDAO provides turnkey on-chain governance infrastructure with built-in multi-role permissions. [near-daos]
- Non-custodial staking: Delegated tokens never leave protocol custody. [near-staking]

**Weaknesses:**
- Illiquidity risk: Locking tokens for maximum veNEAR voting power prevents exiting during severe market volatility. [curve-venear]
- Voter apathy: Like most decentralized governance systems, quorum can be difficult to reach without active foundation participation. [near-governance]

## What the lesson must show

1. View a live or recorded governance proposal (e.g. validator fee adjustment or community treasury grant).
2. The veNEAR calculator: Move a slider between 1 month and 4 years; watch voting power multiplier scale from $1\times$ to $4\times$.
3. Staking delegation overview: Inspect a list of validators, their commission rates, and uptime.
4. Cast a vote on the proposal (or simulate in demo mode).
5. Watch the vote tally update against the required quorum threshold.
