# Lesson 6.1: Read and vote on a proposal

Issue: #40. Target time: 5 minutes. Chain access: read-only or demo mode (optional testnet delegation). Views: 2D and 3D, one shared state.

Facts come from `docs/lessons/stage-6/6.1-topic.md`. Anything else is marked (unverified).

## 1. Learning goals

1. Explain how locking NEAR gives veNEAR voting power: 1:1 at once, plus extra that grows with time locked.
2. Inspect an on-chain DAO proposal and understand quorum requirements.
3. Cast a vote on a governance proposal and observe tally changes.

## 2. Steps

### Step 1: Who decides how NEAR evolves?
- **Caption:** Decentralized networks need a way to make decisions without a CEO or board of directors.
- **Explain:** On NEAR, protocol parameters, treasury grants, and ecosystem incentives are decided through decentralized governance. Token holders, validators, and DAOs participate in steering the network.
- **Scene:** The NEAR community town hall: delegators, validators, and DAO councils. Next is open.

### Step 2: Staking and the unbonding wait
- **Caption:** Delegating your NEAR to a validator earns rewards and contributes to network security.
- **Explain:** You can delegate your tokens to any validator. The validator helps make blocks, and you earn rewards of a few percent a year. If you choose to unstake, funds unlock after **4 epochs**, a little over a day.
- **Scene:** Staking pool mailbox: coins deposited safely behind the validator post. Next is open.

### Step 3: veNEAR: locked NEAR that grows a louder voice
- **Caption:** Why should long-term believers have a louder voice than day traders?
- **Explain:** The **House of Stake** is NEAR's token-holder governance. You lock NEAR and get **veNEAR** (vote-escrow NEAR), its voting power. There is no lock length to pick. 100 NEAR locked gives 100 veNEAR at once. While it stays locked, extra veNEAR grows: on mainnet, 50% of the locked NEAR a year, and the docs say it stops at 4 years. Unlock any amount and you lose all the extra, and the NEAR waits 45 days before you can take it out.
- **Action:** Slider: "Time locked (0 to 4 years)". 100 NEAR is locked; watch the extra veNEAR grow from 0 to 200 (100 to 300 veNEAR in all). A line under it shows what unlocking now would cost.

### Step 4: Inspect a governance proposal
- **Caption:** Review Proposal #42: "Allocate 50,000 NEAR for Open Source Tooling Grants".
- **Explain:** Read the proposer's summary, requested funds, target address, and required quorum. Notice the current tally: 62% Yes, 38% No.
- **Action:** Review proposal details. Next unlocks when reviewed.

### Step 5: Cast your vote
- **Caption:** Cast your weighted vote. This is a practice vote: it changes this page only.
- **Explain:** Your vote counts with the veNEAR you set on step 3. On a real DAO, each vote is a transaction recorded on chain. This practice vote sends nothing; it moves this page's tally and quorum bar.
- **Action:** Buttons "Vote YES" or "Vote NO". Tally updates immediately.
- **Evidence:** `{ proposalId: 42, voteCast: true }` (as built: plus `choice`, `power` and `kind: "practice"`; nothing is sent to a chain).

### Step 6: Quick check (summary page)
- **Caption:** Three questions on veNEAR, staking delegation, and DAOs.
- **Action:** Check (section 3).

### Step 7: Honest take
- **Caption:** Honest take: On-chain governance vs rough consensus.
- **Gate:** Gated until the vote is cast in step 5.

## 3. Check

**Q1. What is the primary purpose of the veNEAR (vote-escrow) model?**
- A. To give more voting power to people who keep their NEAR locked longer. **(correct)**
- B. To force users to pay extra fees to validators. *Wrong: veNEAR is a governance mechanism, not a fee.*
- C. To burn 50% of all liquid NEAR tokens. *Wrong: locking does not burn tokens: you can unlock them (after a 45-day wait).*

**Q2. When you unstake your delegated tokens from a validator, when can you spend them?**
- A. After an unbonding period of 4 epochs, a little over a day. **(correct)**
- B. Immediately within 1 block. *Wrong: Proof-of-Stake security requires an unbonding delay.*
- C. Never; staked tokens are permanently locked. *Wrong: staked tokens are fully refundable.*

**Q3. A trader borrows 1,000,000 NEAR for 10 minutes to sway a House of Stake vote. What stops them?**
- A. Locked NEAR takes 45 days to unlock, so they cannot get it back out to repay a 10-minute loan. **(correct)**
- B. Nothing: the richest account always wins. *Wrong: locked borrowed NEAR would count 1:1 at once, but it would stay stuck for 45 days, long after the loan is due.*
- C. Yes, because loans bypass smart contracts. *Wrong: blockchain rules apply equally to all.*

## 4. Honest take

**Strengths:**
- **Hard to rent a vote:** voting power needs locked NEAR, and locked NEAR takes 45 days to come out, so a short loan cannot vote and be repaid. Power also grows with time locked, which favours people who stay. [hos-contracts] [mainnet-venear]
- **Transparent execution:** Approved DAO proposals execute automatic on-chain transfers directly from treasury smart contracts. [near-daos]

**Weaknesses:**
- **Slow to exit:** more voting power costs you the waiting period. Unlocking takes 45 days, so you cannot react quickly to a market crash or an emergency, and unlocking any amount wipes all the extra veNEAR you built up. [hos-contracts] [mainnet-venear]
- **Low voter turnout:** Reaching quorum often requires significant community rallying, with retail voters frequently inactive. [near-governance]

Compared with: **Ethereum** (informal off-chain governance / EIP consensus without token voting) and **Cosmos** (direct staked token voting with 21-day unbonding).

## 6. Decisions

- **No recorded real DAO proposal.** The topic asks for "a live or recorded governance proposal". The lesson uses Proposal #42, an example made up for the lesson and labelled so on the page. Reading a real House of Stake or Sputnik DAO proposal through the `Chain` interface is a follow-up.
- **No validator list.** The topic's "inspect a list of validators, their commission rates, and uptime" is left out of this lesson; step 2 shows one example pool. A live validator list is a follow-up.
- **Numbers are mainnet's live settings.** 50% a year growth and the 45-day unlock wait are read from `venear.dao` on mainnet (13 Nov 2026). The contract's owner can change them by upgrade; the lesson says "on mainnet".
