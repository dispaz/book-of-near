# Lesson 6.1: Read and vote on a proposal

Issue: #40. Target time: 5 minutes. Chain access: read-only or demo mode (optional testnet delegation). Views: 2D and 3D, one shared state.

Facts come from `docs/lessons/stage-6/6.1-topic.md`. Anything else is marked (unverified).

## 1. Learning goals

1. Explain how token locking creates weighted voting power via veNEAR.
2. Inspect an on-chain DAO proposal and understand quorum requirements.
3. Cast a vote on a governance proposal and observe tally changes.

## 2. Steps

### Step 1: Who decides how NEAR evolves?
- **Caption:** Decentralized networks need a way to make decisions without a CEO or board of directors.
- **Explain:** On NEAR, protocol parameters, treasury grants, and ecosystem incentives are decided through decentralized governance. Token holders, validators, and DAOs participate in steering the network.
- **Scene:** The NEAR community town hall: delegators, validators, and DAO councils. Next is open.

### Step 2: Staking and the 48-hour unbonding rule
- **Caption:** Delegating your NEAR to a validator earns rewards and contributes to network security.
- **Explain:** You can delegate your tokens to any validator. The validator votes on blocks, and you earn ~5% yearly rewards. If you choose to unstake, funds unlock after **4 epochs** (~48 hours).
- **Scene:** Staking pool mailbox: coins deposited safely behind the validator post. Next is open.

### Step 3: The veNEAR voting power multiplier
- **Caption:** Why should long-term believers have a louder voice than day traders?
- **Explain:** The **House of Stake** uses **veNEAR** (vote-escrow NEAR). Locking 100 NEAR for 4 years gives you $4\times$ the voting power of someone holding liquid tokens.
- **Action:** Slider: "Lock duration (1 to 48 months)". Watch the voting power multiplier increase from $1\times$ to $4\times$.

### Step 4: Inspect a governance proposal
- **Caption:** Review Proposal #42: "Allocate 50,000 NEAR for Open Source Tooling Grants".
- **Explain:** Read the proposer's summary, requested funds, target address, and required quorum. Notice the current tally: 62% Yes, 38% No.
- **Action:** Review proposal details. Next unlocks when reviewed.

### Step 5: Cast your vote
- **Caption:** Cast your weighted vote. Every vote is recorded cryptographically on-chain.
- **Explain:** Your vote uses your calculated veNEAR power. Once cast, the quorum bar advances towards the finish line.
- **Action:** Buttons "Vote YES" or "Vote NO". Tally updates immediately.
- **Evidence:** `{ proposalId: 42, voteCast: true }`.

- **Action:** Check (section 3).

## 3. Check

**Q1. What is the primary purpose of the veNEAR (vote-escrow) model?**
- A. To give more voting power to stakeholders who commit to locking their tokens longer. **(correct)**
- B. To force users to pay extra fees to validators. *Wrong: veNEAR is a governance mechanism, not a fee.*
- C. To burn 50% of all liquid NEAR tokens. *Wrong: locking does not burn tokens.*

**Q2. When you unstake your delegated tokens from a validator, when can you spend them?**
- A. After an unbonding period of 4 epochs (~48 hours). **(correct)**
- B. Immediately within 1 block. *Wrong: Proof-of-Stake security requires an unbonding delay.*
- C. Never; staked tokens are permanently locked. *Wrong: staked tokens are fully refundable.*

**Q3. If a wealthy trader borrows 1,000,000 NEAR for 10 minutes, can they control veNEAR governance?**
- A. No, because maximum veNEAR voting power requires locking tokens for multiple years. **(correct)**
- B. Yes, because the richest address always automatically wins. *Wrong: lock duration heavily weights power.*
- C. Yes, because loans bypass smart contracts. *Wrong: blockchain rules apply equally to all.*

## 4. Honest take

**Strengths:**
- **Alignment with long-term health:** Prevents short-term flash loan governance exploits by requiring time-locked commitments. [curve-venear]
- **Transparent execution:** Approved DAO proposals execute automatic on-chain transfers directly from treasury smart contracts. [near-daos]

**Weaknesses:**
- **Capital lockup:** Committing to multi-year locks prevents reacting to sudden market volatility or emergencies. [curve-venear]
- **Low voter turnout:** Reaching quorum often requires significant community rallying, with retail voters frequently inactive. [near-governance]

Compared with: **Ethereum** (informal off-chain governance / EIP consensus without token voting) and **Cosmos** (direct staked token voting with 21-day unbonding).
