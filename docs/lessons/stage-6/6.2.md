## 1. Learning goals

1. Navigate the primary financial building blocks of NEAR DeFi: DEXs, stablecoins, lending, and oracles.
2. Observe how Pyth Network pull oracles deliver real-time asset pricing to smart contracts.
3. Understand why native stablecoins (USDC/USDT) protect capital from bridge vulnerabilities.

## 2. Steps

### Step 1: The engine of commerce
- **Caption:** What turns a blockchain into an economy? Stable money, exchange markets, loans, and price feeds.
- **Explain:** Decentralized finance (DeFi) allows anyone to trade, borrow, and lend without a central bank. On NEAR, these pieces coordinate to provide liquidity for users, developers, and autonomous AI agents.
- **Scene:** The 4 quadrants of the money map lighting up: Stablecoins, DEXs, Lending, Oracles. Next is open.

### Step 2: Native stablecoins vs wrapped IOUs
- **Caption:** Canonical USDC and USDT are minted directly on NEAR by Circle and Tether.
- **Explain:** Many blockchains rely on "wrapped" stablecoins issued by cross-chain bridges. If the bridge is hacked, the tokens become worthless. On NEAR, Circle and Tether issue native tokens backed 1:1 by real dollars in bank reserves.
- **Scene:** Mailbox holding native USDC with a Circle verification seal. Next is open.

### Step 3: Inspect the Pyth price oracle
- **Caption:** Smart contracts cannot fetch Web2 APIs. Oracles deliver verified off-chain prices.
- **Explain:** When a lending app needs to know the price of NEAR, it reads a cryptographic update published by the **Pyth Network**.
- **Action:** Button "Fetch live oracle price". Displays: NEAR/USD price, confidence range ($\pm \$0.002$), and publisher signatures.

### Step 4: Execute a low-slippage stable swap
- **Caption:** Swap 100 USDC for USDT on Ref Finance using a stable-swap pool.
- **Explain:** Stable-swap algorithms use custom curves ($x + y$) rather than standard constant product formulas ($x \times y = k$) to ensure paired dollar tokens swap 1:1 with minimal slippage.
- **Action:** Button "Simulate swap". Shows input: 100.00 USDC, output: 99.98 USDT, fee: 0.02%.
- **Evidence:** `{ pair: "USDC/USDT", slippageBps: 2 }`.

### Step 5: Intent settlement volume
- **Caption:** See where cross-chain capital enters: NEAR is the settlement clearinghouse.
- **Explain:** Notice how the cross-chain intents explored in Stage 4 settle against these liquidity pools. Solvers pull liquidity directly from Ref Finance and native stablecoin reserves.
- **Scene:** Live animated arrows showing cross-chain intent flows routing into the money map. Next is open.

## 3. Check

**Q1. What is the key advantage of native USDC on NEAR over wrapped USDC.e?**
- A. Native USDC is issued directly by Circle and carries zero bridge counterparty risk. **(correct)**
- B. Native USDC generates 100% interest automatically every day. *Wrong: stablecoins maintain dollar pegs, not high inflation.*
- C. Native USDC is mined with Bitcoin miners. *Wrong: stablecoins are backed by fiat reserves, not PoW.*

**Q2. How do smart contracts on NEAR read real-world asset prices?**
- A. By reading cryptographically signed price feeds delivered by decentralized oracles like Pyth. **(correct)**
- B. By making standard HTTP requests to Google Finance. *Wrong: smart contracts cannot make outbound Web2 HTTP requests.*
- C. By asking users to vote on the price every 5 seconds. *Wrong: oracles automate price delivery.*

**Q3. Why do stable-swap AMM pools have lower slippage than standard DEX pools?**
- A. They use specialized math tailored specifically for assets pegged to the same value (1:1). **(correct)**
- B. They ban all selling. *Wrong: stable-swap pools facilitate two-way trading.*
- C. They execute off-chain in private bank databases. *Wrong: all swaps execute on-chain.*

## 4. Honest take

**Strengths:**
- **Rock-solid native stablecoins:** Native Circle USDC and Tether USDT eliminate catastrophic bridge failure risks. [near-stablecoins]
- **Negligible transaction fees:** Swaps and oracle updates cost pennies, enabling high-frequency algorithmic trading. [ref-finance]
- **Sub-second oracle updates:** Pyth provides real-time financial market data. [pyth-network]

**Weaknesses:**
- **Smaller total liquidity (TVL):** Total capital locked in NEAR DeFi is significantly smaller than Ethereum L1 and Solana, limiting multi-million dollar single-tx whale volume. [near-defi]
- **Legacy wrapped token confusion:** Senders must pay attention not to confuse native `USDC` with older bridged `USDC.e`. [near-stablecoins]

Compared with: **Ethereum** (deepest capital pools, but expensive gas and MEV) and **Solana** (high liquidity and speed, but historical network halts).
