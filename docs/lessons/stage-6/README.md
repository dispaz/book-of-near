# Stage 6: Citizen (Ecosystem, Governance and DeFi)

## Stage card intro

Become an active citizen of the NEAR ecosystem. In about fifteen minutes you will explore how decentralized governance coordinates protocol upgrades, see how token locking translates into voting power via the vote-escrow model (veNEAR and the House of Stake), and navigate the live financial topography of NEAR DeFi—from native stablecoins and DEXs to decentralized oracle networks.

## Learning arc

| Lesson | Big idea | What the learner does | Sets up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **6.1 Read and vote on a proposal** | Governance on NEAR combines protocol delegation with vote-escrowed token locking (veNEAR) and on-chain DAOs (AstroDAO). | Inspects a live or recorded governance proposal, examines voting power formulas, and casts a simulated or testnet vote. | "Where does the economic activity governed by these proposals actually flow?" |
| **6.2 The live money map** | NEAR's financial ecosystem comprises native stablecoins, automated market makers (Rhea/Ref), lending markets, and Pyth oracles. | Explores real-time liquidity pools, traces oracle price updates, and tracks cross-chain intent settlement volumes. | Stage 7 (the Capstone: tracing an intent end to end). |

## Notes for builders

- Governance models: Staking delegation, House of Stake, veNEAR, and Sputnik/AstroDAOs.
- DeFi components: Ref Finance (Rhea), Burrow lending, Pyth price feeds, native USDC and USDT.
