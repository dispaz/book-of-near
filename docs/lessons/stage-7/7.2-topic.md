## In one sentence

The Honest Scoreboard provides an unvarnished, objective comparison of NEAR against Ethereum, Solana, and Cosmos across decentralization, scalability, developer experience, and user safety, candidly highlighting where NEAR leads and where it lags.

## The problem it solves

Most blockchain documentation and educational courses suffer from tribal hype: every project claims to be the fastest, most decentralized, and best at everything. Honest learners and developers need realistic trade-offs, clear technical comparisons, and an acknowledgment of genuine weak spots. [bon-spec]

## Detailed Metric Breakdown

### 1. Account Model & Onboarding
- **NEAR:** Leads. Human-readable account names natively, multiple access keys per account, function-call keys with gas allowances, and native meta-transactions without smart contract wallets. [near-keys] [near-account-id]
- **Ethereum:** Hex addresses (`0x...`). Account abstraction (ERC-4337) and EIP-7702 require complex contract wallets and bundlers. [eth-accounts]
- **Solana:** Base58 public key addresses. Programs manage token accounts via Associated Token Accounts (ATAs), which adds friction. [solana-docs]

### 2. Scalability & Sharding Architecture
- **NEAR:** Dynamic Nightshade sharding with Stateless Validation (Nightshade 2.0). Shards scale horizontally without user-visible partitions. [near-sharding]
- **Ethereum:** Danksharding roadmap focuses solely on data availability (blobs) for rollups, not execution sharding. [eth-danksharding]
- **Solana:** Single-state multi-threaded pipelining (Sealevel). Fast, but requires high-end server hardware: Anza's validator requirements list "12 cores / 24 threads, or more" at 2.8 GHz or faster. [solana-docs] [anza-hardware]

### 3. Execution Model & Latency
- **Solana:** Sub-second block times (~400ms) with synchronous execution across accounts. [solana-docs] (unverified: the ~400 ms figure could not be re-read on 13 Nov 2026, solana.com rate-limited us and Anza's docs give slot length only in PoH ticks. The lesson marks it unverified.)
- **Ethereum:** 12-second slots ("slots (12 seconds)" [eth-pos]), synchronous atomic EVM call stack. Reentrancy vulnerabilities exist. [eth-evm]
- **NEAR:** a block every ~0.6 to 1 s (the docs give both: "around 600ms" [near-finality], "approximately every second" [near-gas]). Asynchronous receipt hops eliminate reentrancy, but multi-contract calls require multiple blocks to complete. [near-cross-contract]

### 4. Ecosystem Liquidity & Network Effects
- **Ethereum:** Decisive leader in Total Value Locked (TVL), institutional capital, liquidity depth, and developer mindshare. [eth-defi]
- **Solana:** Massive retail volume, high DEX turnover, dominant memecoin mindshare. [solana-defi]
- **NEAR:** Smaller native DeFi TVL, but acts as a multichain liquidity routing layer via NEAR Intents and Chain Signatures. [near-intents]

## Words to know

- **Monolithic Blockchain:** A chain that handles consensus, execution, and data availability on a single un-partitioned layer (e.g. Solana).
- **Modular Blockchain:** A chain that separates execution (rollups) from consensus and settlement (e.g. Ethereum L1 + Arbitrum).
- **Sharded Blockchain:** A chain that partitions state and execution horizontally into parallel tracks unified by one protocol (e.g. NEAR).
- **Chain Abstraction:** The design paradigm that hides blockchain infrastructure, gas tokens, and bridges entirely from user experience.

## Strengths and weaknesses summary

**Where NEAR Wins:**
1. **User Experience:** Named accounts, scoped keys, and zero-bridge intents.
2. **Horizontal Scaling:** Nightshade sharding scales validator workloads statelessly.
3. ~~**Developer Economics:** 30% of gas fees permanently rebated to contract creators.~~ **Out of date:** the NEAR docs now say "On protocol version 86 and earlier, contracts also receive 30% of the gas they burn while executing. From protocol version 87 (nearcore 2.14.0), all of it is burned." [near-gas] The lesson does not teach the rebate.
4. **Multichain Reach:** Chain Signatures control Bitcoin, Ethereum, and Solana natively.

**Where NEAR Trails:**
1. **Capital Depth:** Ethereum holds drastically more DeFi capital and institutional custody.
2. **Synchronous Atomicity:** Cross-contract calls take multiple blocks and cannot roll back atomically across receipts.
3. **Brand Mindshare:** Less retail speculative frenzy compared to Ethereum and Solana.
