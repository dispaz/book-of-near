# Stage 7: Book of NEAR (Capstone and Honest Scoreboard)

## Stage card intro

The grand finale. In this capstone stage you will synthesize everything you have learned across the entire book. You will trace one real cross-chain intent from start to finish across every layer of the NEAR protocol, weigh NEAR objectively against Ethereum, Solana, and Cosmos in the Honest Scoreboard, take the final route-based exam, and claim your verified Book of NEAR NFT Badge.
