# Lesson 7.1: Trace one intent end to end

Issue: #26. Target time: 6 minutes. Chain access: testnet or demo mode (mints stage/final badge). Views: 2D and 3D, one shared state.

Facts come from `docs/lessons/stage-7/7.1-topic.md`. Anything else is marked (unverified).

## 1. Learning goals

1. Trace a single cross-chain intent through every layer of NEAR: identity, scoped keys, sharded receipts, Wasm execution, and MPC Chain Signatures.
2. Experience how all previous stages compose into a unified Chain Abstraction experience.
3. Record capstone evidence to qualify for the Book of NEAR Final Exam and NFT Badge.

## 2. Steps

### Step 1: The grand synthesis
- **Caption:** Every concept you learned is part of one engine. Watch them coordinate to move money across chains.
- **Explain:** You started with basic ledgers and keys. Now you will trace a single real cross-chain intent through all 5 layers: Account, Scoped Key, Shards, Smart Contracts, and Chain Signatures.
- **Scene:** The 5 layers standing as layered plates in 3D. Next is open.

### Step 2: Layer 1 & 2: Identity and the scoped key
- **Caption:** Your named account ({name}) signs the intent with a restricted function-call key.
- **Explain:** You don't risk your master private key. The function-call key created in Stage 1 can only interact with the Intent Verifier contract and is capped by your gas allowance.
- **Action:** Button "Authorize intent". The stamped parcel leaves your mailbox. Next unlocks when signed.

### Step 3: Layer 3: Nightshade shards and asynchronous receipts
- **Caption:** The parcel enters your shard's chunk and emits an Action Receipt across blocks.
- **Explain:** Nightshade routes the execution to the Intent Verifier's shard. Validators verify the chunk statelessly using cryptographic state witnesses, with zero global locks.
- **Scene:** Visualizing the delivery slip hopping across shard boundaries into the verifier contract scroll. Next is open.

### Step 4: Layer 4: Wasm execution, storage staking, and 30% rebate
- **Caption:** The contract executes inside the Wasm runtime; 30% of gas burned is rebated to the creator.
- **Explain:** The Intent Verifier contract checks parameters. Its storage is backed by refundable storage staking (NEP-145). The protocol automatically redirects 30% of gas fees to the contract developer's balance.
- **Scene:** The scroll gear spins; gas ink drops split 70% burn / 30% developer mailbox. Next is open.

### Step 5: Layer 5: Solvers and MPC Chain Signatures
- **Caption:** Competing solvers fulfill the order; the MPC network generates a threshold signature.
- **Explain:** The winning solver delivers native USDC on Arbitrum. To settle the payout, NEAR's decentralized MPC validator ring signs the cross-chain authorization in ~5 seconds with 1 yocto deposit.
- **Action:** Button "Verify foreign settlement". The foreign transaction finalizes on destination.
- **Evidence:** `{ capstoneComplete: true, txHash, mpcVerified: true }`.

- **Action:** Check (section 3).

## 3. Check

**Q1. Why does the learner use a function-call key to authorize the intent?**
- A. So that even if the client application is compromised, the root account and main funds remain safe. **(correct)**
- B. Because full-access keys cannot connect to the internet. *Wrong: full-access keys can do everything.*
- C. To avoid paying taxes on crypto. *Wrong: access key types are technical security primitives.*

**Q2. How do Nightshade shards handle cross-contract calls without locking the blockchain?**
- A. Through asynchronous Action and Data receipts that execute across block boundaries. **(correct)**
- B. By stopping all transactions on all other shards. *Wrong: shards process chunks in parallel.*
- C. By sending emails between node operators. *Wrong: routing is automated by consensus.*

**Q3. How does NEAR deliver native assets on foreign chains without lock-and-mint bridge honeypots?**
- A. Solvers front native liquidity on destination, and NEAR MPC Chain Signatures verify settlement. **(correct)**
- B. By printing counterfeit Bitcoin tokens. *Wrong: assets delivered are authentic native coins.*
- C. By shutting down foreign blockchains. *Wrong: foreign chains run normally.*

## 4. Honest take

**Strengths:**
- **True Chain Abstraction:** Solves multichain user experience from identity to cross-chain settlement with zero bridge honeypots. [near-intents]
- **Protocol harmony:** Identity (named accounts), security (scoped keys), scalability (Nightshade), and multichain reach (Chain Signatures) work as a single native engine. [near-chain-sigs]

**Weaknesses:**
- **End-to-end latency:** A complete intent cycle spanning shard hops and MPC threshold signing takes 6–10 seconds. [spike-5]
- **Multi-system complexity:** Diagnosing a failure requires tracing nonces, receipt hops, and solver liquidity proofs. [near-exec]

Compared with: **Ethereum + L2 Ecosystem** (fragmented rollups, bridge risks, disparate gas tokens) and **Solana** (fast single state, but no native sharding or multichain MPC signatures).
