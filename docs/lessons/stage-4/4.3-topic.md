# Topic 4.3: Classic Bridges vs. The NEAR Intent Way

## In one sentence

While classic lock-and-mint bridges trap billions in vulnerable contract honeypots and issue fragile synthetic wrapped tokens, NEAR replaces bridges with intent-based settlement that delivers native assets in seconds.

## The problem it solves

Over $2.8 billion has been stolen in cross-chain bridge hacks across the crypto ecosystem (e.g. Ronin, Wormhole, Nomad). Traditional bridges lock collateral inside centralized or multi-sig smart contracts, creating irresistible targets for hackers. When a bridge is exploited, its synthetic "wrapped tokens" instantly collapse to zero, wiping out unsuspecting users. [bridge-hacks] [near-bridge-risk]

## How it works on NEAR

**The Classic Lock-and-Mint Model.**
1. **Deposit:** User deposits native USDC into a Bridge Vault contract on Ethereum. [near-bridge-risk]
2. **Attestation:** A set of bridge validators or multi-sig signers observes the deposit. [near-bridge-risk]
3. **Minting:** The bridge contract on the destination chain mints a synthetic IOU token (e.g. `wUSDC` or `USDC.e`). [near-bridge-risk]
4. **Vulnerability:** The funds in the source vault form a massive honeypot. If the multi-sig keys leak or the contract has a bug, all wrapped tokens lose their backing. [bridge-hacks]

**The NEAR Intent & Chain Signature Model.**
1. **Zero User Honeypot:** The user never deposits into a permanent, multi-million-dollar shared bridge vault. [near-intents]
2. **Native Asset Delivery:** Solvers deliver real, native assets (e.g. native USDC on Arbitrum or native BTC on Bitcoin) directly to the user's destination account. [near-intents]
3. **Inventory Risk on Solvers:** If a transaction is delayed or an underlying chain re-organizes, the professional market maker (solver) absorbs the inventory risk, not the user. [near-intents]
4. **Confidential Intents:** Advanced privacy-preserving intent extensions encrypt trade specifics off-chain, preventing arbitrageurs and front-runners from observing trade sizes before execution. [near-intents]

## Words to know

- **Lock-and-Mint Bridge:** A cross-chain protocol that locks native assets on one chain to mint representative wrapped tokens on another.
- **Wrapped Token (Synthetic Asset):** An IOU token representing an underlying asset held in escrow elsewhere.
- **Honeypot:** A massive pool of locked capital in a smart contract that attracts malicious attacks.
- **Native Asset:** The canonical token issued directly on its home blockchain (e.g. native USDC vs wrapped USDC.e).
- **Confidential Intent:** An intent encrypted and processed in confidential execution environments to hide trade data until fulfillment.

## Common misunderstandings

- **"Wrapped tokens are as safe as the native coin."** No. If the bridge holding the native asset is hacked, the wrapped token becomes completely unbacked and worthless. [near-bridge-risk]
- **"Intent systems eliminate all risk."** Intent systems eliminate smart contract bridge honeypots and user execution risk, but rely on active market maker liquidity and the cryptographic integrity of the settlement verifier. [near-intents]

## Compared with Traditional Bridges

- **Traditional Bridges (Wormhole, LayerZero, Multichain):** Rely on relayer networks and smart contract pools holding locked user collateral. Users must deal with wrapped assets and delayed finality. [near-bridge-risk]
- **NEAR Intents:** Peer-to-peer economic fulfillment with solvers; users receive native assets with no permanent lock-and-mint pool. [near-intents]

## Strengths and weaknesses (feeds the Honest take)

**Strengths:**
- Eliminates bridge honeypots: No multi-million-dollar collateral pools waiting to be drained. [near-intents]
- Native assets only: Users receive canonical tokens rather than fragmented bridge IOUs. [near-intents]
- Speed: Solvers fulfill orders within seconds rather than waiting for multi-block cross-chain consensus confirmations. [near-intents]

**Weaknesses:**
- Solver fee spread: Solvers charge a fractional spread or fee for taking on capital inventory and volatility risk. [spike-6]
- Capital efficiency for solvers: Market makers must maintain pre-funded capital pools across multiple destination networks. [near-intents]
