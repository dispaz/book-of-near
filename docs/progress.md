## 2026-11-09 · Book scene camera: gentle glides, a small pan, the learner's view kept, Controls card (Refs #17)

- Learner feedback while trying lesson 2.1: the camera could only orbit and zoom, every view change felt twitchy, and moving the camera was undone by the next step. On its own branch so main gets it before 2.1.
- `CameraRig`: pan on (right drag, shift/ctrl/cmd + drag, two-finger drag), held within 15 % of the camera's distance (`ORBIT.pan`) around where the learner took over. Softer controls (rotate 0.6, zoom 0.7, pan 0.6, damping 0.06).
- Camera and the picture's view offset glide on one critically damped spring (`smoothDamp`, `EASE_SECONDS` 0.45 s, frame-rate independent) instead of per-frame lerps (0.06 camera, 0.1 offset) that started with a jolt and ran twice as fast on 120 Hz screens. A goal change mid-glide bends the path. `data-camera="still"` waits for the view offset too. `Traveller` trail rates scale with frame time.
- Book scenes keep the learner's view (`InkStage keepView`): once the camera moves under their hand (a plain click does not count), steps no longer reframe it; Reset view, Flat view or the tour hand it back. It survives a 2D/3D switch, in memory only.
- Camera cluster (`CameraControls`) top right of the free area: Reset view (while their view is on) and Controls, a disclosure card for mouse, touch (own pointer first) and keyboard. It does not open by itself on a first visit, so it never covers the page or a lesson's first action.
- Tests: `smoothDamp` / `clampLength` / `helpFor` unit tests; `e2e/book-camera.spec.ts` on lesson 0.1 (a click is not a move, view kept across a step, Reset view, pan clamp, Controls open / Esc / outside, phone fit). Test hooks on `.sk-stage`: `data-camera-user`, `data-camera-pose`.
- Not done: keyboard camera keys (the canvas is not a focus stop; arrow keys belong to the page).
- Review fixes (PR #38):
  - The kept view is keyed by the lesson id (`LessonIdContext` → `BookScene viewMemory`, captured at mount, `viewMemory.ts`), not the URL. Before, it leaked into the next lesson under the data router and was shared by every lesson under hash routing.
  - A press counts as the learner's view only past `CLICK_PX` or on a zoom / two fingers (`isCameraGesture`), so a drifting tap picks without freezing the camera. A press no longer stops a running glide dead.
  - Returning to 3D with a kept view skips the top-down intro press, which was flattening the view; the objects still grow.
  - Reset view moves focus to Controls. Orbit damping is frame-rate independent (`dampingFor`). One `MAX_FRAME_STEP` (0.1 s) for the camera, view offset and Traveller (the Traveller was 1/20). `far()` uses the shared `distance`. The pan reach shrinks when zoomed in.
- Re-review fixes (PR #38): `data-camera` turns `moving` in the same tick a glide, reset or press is requested (a test read the old pose after Reset view, 1 in 81 runs). `Pickable` measures the press's furthest travel, as the rig does, so out 7 px and back is a move, not also a pick. The pan is anchored where the target is at take-over (a glide may move it after the press begins).
- Pan anywhere over the open book (user feedback: the right page could not be brought to the middle): in book scenes the target is clamped per axis to `bookFootprint(spread)` (both pages plus max(0.5, 5 % of the spread)) and slides across the desk at its height, instead of 15 % of the distance around the take-over point. Other 3D scenes keep the radius. Checked at 1440 × 900 on lesson 0.1: the right page centres in the free area; the far-right stop is the book's edge.
- Merged main (#39, camera limits and the room): one rig. #39's 45° / 1.3× limits (`ORBIT`, `orbitLimits`) hold on our spring rig, relaxed while it glides; their grab-mid-glide rule lives in our take-over (the range centres on the step's view if the camera is there, else on where it is at take-over). Once the learner takes over, the range stays centred on the view they took it from through later steps until Reset view; the kept view carries that centre. `PAN_SHARE` became `ORBIT.pan`. The Controls card says turns go up to 45° either way.
- Merge review fixes: the camera can no longer go through the room's side wall (pan to the book's edge, turn, tilt down, zoom out reached x 30.98 past a wall at 24.98 on lesson 0.1). The rig keeps it inside the room the backdrop draws (`roomForSpread`, `cameraRoom`, 2 units clear), coming nearer along its line of sight (`keepInside`). Unit test: every pan corner × turn × tilt × zoom out to 140, for lesson 0.1's spreads and a small and a wide one, ends inside.
