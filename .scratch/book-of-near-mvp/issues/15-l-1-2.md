# 15: Lesson 1.2: A key that can do only one thing

**What to build:** The learner adds a key limited to one contract with a small allowance and hands it to a scripted agent (no AI model calls). The learner tries prompts such as "send all funds" and "call another contract" and watches the chain refuse each attempt, then deletes the key. A no-hint mode records whether the learner finished without hints, for the final badge rarity. Ends with an honest take comparing scoped keys with Ethereum accounts that have one key.
