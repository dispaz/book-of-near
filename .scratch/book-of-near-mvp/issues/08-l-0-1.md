# 08: Lesson 0.1: How a blockchain and a transaction work

**What to build:** A short interactive lesson (under five minutes). The learner moves coins between three accounts on a toy shared ledger, watches blocks form and link together, edits an old block and sees the hashes break, and answers a short check. Ends with an honest take about what blockchains are bad at.

**Blocked by:** 03 (3D scene kit v1), 05 (Opening quiz, routes, progress and table of contents)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Priority:** P1
