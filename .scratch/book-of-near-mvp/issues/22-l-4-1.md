# 22: Lesson 4.1: One account, every chain (3D)

**What to build:** A large 3D showpiece. The learner derives an Ethereum address from their NEAR account, then signs and sends a Sepolia transaction through the signing network. The scene shows the NEAR account, the signing nodes and Ethereum. Uses the library calls and amounts proven in the spikes; if broadcast cannot be made reliable, shows the signed payload and derived address without broadcasting. Ends with an honest take on Chain Signatures compared with bridges and smart wallets.
