# 21: Lesson 3.3: Race the RPC nodes

**What to build:** The learner sends one query to several RPC providers, compares latency bars, then blocks one provider and watches failover. This shows why wallets do not trust a single node and why providers differ from running your own node. Ends with an honest take on RPC reliability.
