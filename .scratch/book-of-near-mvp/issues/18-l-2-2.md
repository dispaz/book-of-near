# 18: Lesson 2.2: A call that takes several blocks

**What to build:** The learner calls a contract that calls another contract on a different shard and watches the receipts hop between blocks, with gas burned and the 30% paid to the called contract shown. Uses two helper accounts the spikes proved sit on different shards. Ends with an honest take on asynchronous calls compared with Ethereum's synchronous ones.
