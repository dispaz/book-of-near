# 17: Lesson 2.1: Follow your transaction across shards (3D)

**What to build:** A large 3D showpiece. The learner picks one of their earlier transactions, the app fetches it over RPC, and the scene traces it through shards, chunks and blocks as 3D blocks, showing how long it took to become final. Where the spike found shard ids hard to read, use the fallback it chose. Ends with an honest take on sharding compared with Ethereum rollups and Solana's single state.
